## Course 1 – Foundations of Cybersecurity

- **Cybersecurity** is protecting networks, devices, people and data from unauthorised access or criminal exploitation.
- **CIA triad:** Confidentiality (only authorised access), Integrity (data is accurate and unaltered), Availability (systems and data are accessible when needed).
- Common threats: phishing, malware, ransomware, social engineering, insider threats.
- Security analysts monitor systems, investigate alerts and help reduce risk.
- Ethics matter: protect privacy, follow laws, act only with authorisation.

## Course 2 – Play It Safe: Manage Security Risks

- **Risk** = threat × vulnerability. A **threat** is anything that can cause harm; a **vulnerability** is a weakness it can exploit.
- Security frameworks and controls give structure. The NIST Cybersecurity Framework groups activities into functions such as Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover.
- **Security controls** are administrative (policies, training), technical (encryption, firewalls) and physical (locks, badges).
- Tools such as a SIEM collect and analyse logs so analysts can spot suspicious activity.
- Playbooks describe the steps to follow for a given incident type.
