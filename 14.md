## 1. Strategies and how they were used

| Strategy | What it means | How it applies to my study |
|---|---|---|
| Active recall | Retrieve information before checking notes | Write an algorithm from memory, then compare |
| Spaced repetition | Review at increasing intervals | Revisit DSA and cybersecurity notes across the week |
| Manageable goals | Break a topic into a small session goal | "Trace merge sort on one array" instead of "study sorting" |
| Reflecting on mistakes | Record errors and the reason | Keep a short list of wrong answers and fixes |
| Pomodoro | 25 minutes focus, short break | Practice problems and note writing |
